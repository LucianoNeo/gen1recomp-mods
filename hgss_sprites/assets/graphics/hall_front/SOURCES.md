# Hall of Fame player portraits

The Hall of Fame portraits are native player trainer sprites sourced from the
Bulbagarden Archives. They are used at their authored pixel dimensions; no
generic or unrelated portrait artwork is substituted.

character rather than a protagonist with a main-series game trainer sprite.
